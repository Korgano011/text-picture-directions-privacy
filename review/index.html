App Review Demo

Text & Picture Directions · Screen recording on iPhone, October 26, 2026

Download the video (MP4, 2 min)

Street names, addresses, nearby places and maps are blurred in this recording for privacy. The app shows them normally.

What the recording shows

  • Launching the app from the Home Screen.
  • Text: typing an address, choosing a suggestion, and getting walking directions (Walk, Drive, Bus, and Train are available).
  • Picture: choosing a photo; the app reads where it was taken, lists nearby places, and gives directions to one.
  • Indoor: Record a Route → Start Recording, walking the route while turns are noted, then I’ve Arrived.
  • Repository: saving directions (with the option to leave out step-by-step directions) and browsing saved directions.

More information

No account or login is needed. See the Privacy Policy for the services the app uses and what is shared.
